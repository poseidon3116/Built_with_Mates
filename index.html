Community Builds

BYO LEGO. Build together.

We're exploring a social group format: afternoon sessions where people come together, bring their own sets, and we all build as a crew. Good vibes, good builds, good people.

🧱 The Concept

Hosted afternoons at one of our hubs. Bring your own current LEGO project (or we provide one). Nik and Jesse facilitate, help troubleshoot, and keep the energy going. Think of it like a build club.

👥 Who It's For

Adults and young people who love LEGO and want a social building experience. Particularly useful for people who find solo building isolating or who want a structured, relaxed social environment.

📅 Format (proposed)

Sunday or Saturday afternoons, ~2–3 hours. Small groups (6–10 people). BYO set or borrow from our collection. Light refreshments. Community-first atmosphere.

NDIS note: We're exploring how this concept could expand into a supported social group for participants with disability, using LEGO as a therapeutic, skills-building, and social activity. If you're a support worker, coordinator, or participant interested in this angle, please mention it in the EOI below. We're in early stages and want to hear from the community first.
